On parking, from your correspondent

  • There's usually street parking around the square. Read the posted signs before you settle in; time limits are a matter of public record, not opinion.
  • If the square is full, public lots tend to turn up a block or two off it. A short walk never hurt a latte.
  • Cyclists: look for bike racks near the square. Walkers can come in from the river trails and arrive with a better story than the drivers.
